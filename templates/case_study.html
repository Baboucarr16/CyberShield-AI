📚 CyberShield AI Case Study

Understanding how CyberShield AI detects and analyzes phishing websites.

🛡️ 1. Introduction

Phishing is one of the most common cybersecurity threats on the internet. Attackers create fake websites that imitate legitimate websites in order to trick users into revealing sensitive information such as passwords, personal information, or financial details.

CyberShield AI is a phishing detection platform designed to analyze website URLs and identify suspicious characteristics that may indicate a phishing attack.

⚠️ 2. Problem Statement

Users often cannot easily determine whether a website is legitimate or malicious. Phishing URLs can look very similar to genuine websites, making them difficult to identify manually.

The problem:
  • Users may click malicious links.
  • Fake websites can look identical to legitimate websites.
  • Suspicious URLs are difficult to identify manually.
  • Users need a quick way to assess website risk.

💡 3. Proposed Solution

CyberShield AI provides an automated website scanning system. The user enters a website URL and the system analyzes the URL for phishing indicators.

🔎 4. Detection Process

CyberShield AI examines different characteristics of a website URL. These characteristics are used to identify suspicious behavior.

🔐

HTTPS

Checks whether the website uses a secure HTTPS connection.

🔗

URL Length

Examines unusually long URLs that may contain suspicious data.

📍

IP Address

Detects URLs that directly use an IP address instead of a domain.

🚨

Suspicious Words

Looks for words commonly associated with phishing attempts.

🔢

Special Characters

Checks suspicious URL characteristics such as symbols and unusual formatting.

🤖

Detection Engine

Combines the analyzed indicators to determine the website risk.

🛡️ 5. Google Safe Browsing

CyberShield AI also checks the scanned URL against Google Safe Browsing to determine whether the URL is already known as an unsafe resource.

🛡️

Additional Security Layer

This provides an additional layer of protection by checking the URL against known threat information.

📊 6. Risk Scoring

After analyzing the website, CyberShield AI produces a risk score between 0 and 10.

0–2

LOW RISK

The website shows few or no suspicious indicators.

3–5

MEDIUM RISK

The website contains some suspicious characteristics.

6–10

HIGH RISK

Multiple suspicious indicators may indicate phishing activity.
