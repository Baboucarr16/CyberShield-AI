About CyberShield AI

AI-Powered Phishing Detection Platform

🛡️ What is CyberShield AI?

CyberShield AI is a web-based phishing detection platform designed to help users identify potentially dangerous websites before visiting them.

The system analyzes website URLs and looks for suspicious characteristics that may indicate phishing activity. It then provides a risk score, security analysis, and a clear recommendation to the user.

🎯 Our Purpose

The main purpose of CyberShield AI is to provide users with a simple and accessible way to check suspicious website URLs.

🔍

Detect

Identify suspicious URL characteristics associated with phishing.

📊

Analyze

Analyze different URL features and security indicators.

🛡️

Protect

Help users make safer decisions before visiting suspicious websites.
